The HundeWildnis app is a mobile companion for everyday life with your dog – from daily training, scent work, vital sign measurement and first aid to maps, routes and weather analysis. All features share the dog profiles.

Your data stays on your device. The app has no tracking, no ads and no Google services.

Features (selection):
• Training diary and scent work with map, route and area calculation
• Local weather with paw-weather index, air quality, pollen and daylight
• Places nearby (dog parks, vets, drinking water and more) and routes
• First aid, emergency report and heat cycle calendar
• PDF export of reports
• Backup via WebDAV (e.g. Nextcloud)

Notes on external services:
The app only fetches data from free services (OpenStreetMap, Overpass, Bright Sky/DWD) when you trigger a feature. For weather you choose a provider in the settings (OpenWeatherMap or Open-Meteo) and enter your own key or server; without it the app shows no weather. For routes you enter your own free OpenRouteService key. You can change the Overpass server in the settings.
